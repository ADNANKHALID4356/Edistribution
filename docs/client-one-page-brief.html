Distribution Management App

One-Page Client Brief (Non-Technical)

Prepared On: April 24, 2026
Audience: Business Stakeholders, Owners, Operational Managers

1) Business Purpose

This app is designed to run the full distribution cycle in one connected system.
It helps the business control products, orders, deliveries, customer dues, payments, and returns from one place.

2) Who Uses It

  • Admin/Manager (Desktop): Runs setup, controls operations, checks reports, and monitors financials.
  • Salesman (Mobile): Visits shops, books orders, and syncs data from the field.
  • Owner/Stakeholder (Desktop): Tracks sales activity, delivery progress, receivables, and collections.

3) How the App Works (Daily Flow)

  1. Company data is set up: products, routes, shops, warehouses, and salesmen.
  2. Salesmen take shop orders from mobile devices during market visits.
  3. Orders are synced to the central system immediately or when internet is available.
  4. Office team reviews and processes orders into deliveries.
  5. Deliveries update stock and customer balances automatically.
  6. Payments are recorded as daily collections and posted to customer ledgers.
  7. If goods are returned, stock and financial balances are adjusted automatically.

4) Core Capabilities

  • Product and stock management by warehouse
  • Route-wise and shop-wise sales operations
  • Salesman assignment and control
  • Order lifecycle management (draft to delivered)
  • Delivery tracking and status visibility
  • Customer ledger and outstanding balance tracking
  • Daily cash/bank collection records
  • Stock return handling with automatic correction entries
  • Route consolidated billing view for settlement support

5) Reliability and Control Features

  • Role-based access so each user sees only relevant actions
  • Salesman-level data separation on mobile
  • Built-in offline mode for poor network conditions
  • Auto-sync and retry system to reduce lost orders
  • Validation checks to prevent duplicate or invalid transactions
  • Dependency checks before critical deletions

6) Business Outcomes

  • Faster order-to-delivery cycle
  • Better stock accuracy and lower manual mistakes
  • Stronger receivables control through customer ledgers
  • Higher field productivity with offline support
  • Better decision-making through dashboard visibility

7) What a Stakeholder Can Monitor Easily

  • Total orders and delivery progress
  • Route-wise and salesman-wise business activity
  • Pending customer balances (dues)
  • Daily collections by payment method
  • Return trends and stock impact

8) Current Process Design Note

The active operational flow is currently Order -> Delivery.
Invoice-related technical components exist but are not the primary active user flow in the current desktop navigation.

9) Summary Statement

This app provides a practical, field-ready, and financially controlled distribution operating system.
It combines sales execution, stock control, and cash recovery into one trackable workflow suitable for day-to-day business management and growth.
