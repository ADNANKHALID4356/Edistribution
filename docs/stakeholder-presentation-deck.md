## Slide 2: Business Challenge
**Title:** What Problem Are We Solving?

**Key Points:**
- Manual and disconnected work causes delays and errors.
- Field order taking is difficult when internet is unstable.
- Delivery, stock, and payment information often do not stay aligned.
- Management lacks one real-time view of operations and receivables.

**Speaker Notes:**  
The app addresses operational fragmentation by bringing field and office teams into one controlled workflow.

---

## Slide 3: Solution Overview
**Title:** What the App Delivers

**Key Points:**
- One platform for products, routes, shops, salesmen, orders, deliveries, and financial tracking.
- Desktop module for admin control and reporting.
- Mobile module for field sales execution.
- Central server to keep official business records synchronized.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Think of this as a business operating system for distribution, not just an order app.

---

## Slide 4: End-to-End Process
**Title:** Daily Operational Flow

**Key Points:**
1. Admin sets up products, routes, shops, warehouses, and salesmen.
2. Salesman books shop orders on mobile during visits.
3. Orders sync instantly or later when internet is available.
4. Office processes orders and manages deliveries.
5. Stock and customer balances update automatically.
6. Payments and returns are recorded with automatic adjustments.

**Speaker Notes:**  
The system links execution and finance so business decisions rely on updated and connected data.

---

## Slide 5: User Roles and Responsibilities
**Title:** Who Uses What

**Key Points:**
- **Admin/Manager (Desktop):** Setup, approvals, delivery control, ledger oversight.
- **Salesman (Mobile):** Shop visits, order booking, route-based execution.
- **Owner/Stakeholder (Desktop):** Sales health, delivery performance, receivable visibility.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Each role gets focused access, reducing confusion and improving accountability.

## Slide 7: Control, Security, and Reliability
**Title:** Built-In Business Controls

**Key Points:**
- Role-based access control
- Salesman-level data isolation
- Offline mobile operation for weak network areas
- Auto-sync with retry for pending transactions
- Validation checks for duplicate and invalid actions
- Dependency safeguards before critical delete actions

**Speaker Notes:**  
These controls reduce operational risk and improve trust in reported data.
